PREPFIX CHURCH MEDIA TOOLKIT - HOW TO USE
=========================================
Developed by Cyrus Ireri - 0720872582

The easy step-by-step guide is  GUIDE - Prepfix Church Media Toolkit.docx  in this folder (toolkit, phone app,
team app). Send  Prepfix Team app - for members.pdf  to your praise and worship team. This file has every detail.

Files in this folder
  server.js          the program (do not edit)
  index.html         the screens (do not edit)
  data.json          ALL your songs, playlists and settings - keep it safe
  data.backup.json   automatic copy of data.json, made every time the server starts
  wallpaper.bin      the church wallpaper picture (appears after you upload one)

Nothing to install: node.exe is inside this folder.


1. START THE APPLICATION
------------------------
  - Open the toolkit folder.
  - Click the address bar at the top of the folder window, type   cmd   and press Enter.
  - In the black window type:   node server.js   and press Enter.
  - Leave that black window open during the whole service. Closing it stops the app.
  - The window shows the addresses to use (see below). To stop: press Ctrl+C or close it.

  Easier: double-click "0 START PREPFIX CHURCH.bat" in this folder (it is always the first file in the list).
  The first time, it also makes a gold-and-blue cross icon called "START PREPFIX CHURCH" in this folder and
  "Prepfix Church" on the Desktop. From then on, double-click either icon.
  It also removes the "Open File - Security Warning" for good: it clears the "downloaded from the internet" mark
  from every file in this folder, and tells Windows not to ask about the files it starts.
  (If the zip itself came from the internet and Windows asks once before the very first start: right-click the
  zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK, then unzip. Or choose "Run" on that one question - it never comes back.)


2. OPEN IT ON THE COMPUTER
--------------------------
  The top line of the control page (Prepfix logo and the church name, glowing) stays fixed while you scroll.
  The control page opens by itself when you start the app. If you close it, go to
  http://localhost:3000 in Chrome or Edge (or double-click "0 START PREPFIX CHURCH.bat" again).


3. OPEN IT ON A PHONE (same Wi-Fi)
----------------------------------
  - Connect the phone to the SAME Wi-Fi as the computer.
  - The black window shows a line like:   Control (phone):  http://192.168.1.20:3000
  - Type that address into the phone's browser.
  - If it does not open: when Windows asks about the firewall the first time, choose "Allow"
    (tick "Private networks"). Make sure the computer's Wi-Fi is set to a Private network.
  - On the phone tap "Songs · Media · Tools" to choose a song and "Back to song" to return.
    "More" shows the other buttons (wallpaper, blank stream, projector, settings).
    The song's Edit / Share / Add to playlist / Delete are in the small "..." menu by its title.
  - At the top: Previous stanza | Hide screen | Next stanza. Tap a line number to show a line.


4. CHURCH PROJECTOR (HDMI)
--------------------------
  - Connect the projector/TV by HDMI and set Windows to "Extend" (Windows key + P).
  - AUTOMATIC: when you start the app, the church screen opens by itself, full screen, on
    the projector, and the control page opens on your computer screen. If the projector
    is connected later, the church screen opens on it within about 10 seconds.
    (Turn this off in Display settings > "Open the church screen on the projector by itself".)
  - Press "Show on projector" (or key P). The church screen opens FULL SCREEN on the
    projector by itself, while your control page stays on the computer screen.
  - To exit, press "Close projector" (or key P again). Press it again to bring it back.
    This works from the phone too.
  - If two extra screens are connected, it asks which one is the projector.
  - "Hide screen" (key H) makes the church window step aside instantly, so the projector
    shows your normal Windows desktop and wallpaper exactly as usual. "Show screen" brings
    the full-screen lyrics straight back (no reopening).
    While hidden you can keep moving through the lines (the stream still follows);
    the projector stays hidden until you press "Show screen".
  - Manual way (any browser): "Open church screen", drag the window to the projector,
    then double-click it (or press F11) for full screen. Esc leaves full screen.
  - Tapping a line shows the WHOLE stanza on the projector. Other lines in the same stanza
    do not change the projector; choosing a line in a different stanza changes it.
  - "Church wallpaper" button (key B) hides the lyrics and shows the wallpaper / background.
  - The church screen shows the church name and the Prepfix logo small in the bottom-right
    corner (Display settings can switch this off). The live stream never shows it.
  - The round church seal (Display settings > "Show the round church seal"): the church name runs round the top of
    the ring from 8 o'clock to 4 o'clock, a row of stars fills the bottom, and a small crucifix (Christ on the cross)
    is in the middle, big enough for Christ to be seen.
  - The same seal shows beside the lyrics on the live stream (OBS). Display settings > Stream screen >
    "Show the church seal (logo) beside the lyrics on the stream" turns it off. It shows only while lyrics show.
  - Church screen preview: the small box in the bottom-right of the control page shows exactly
    what the church screen shows, even when no projector is connected (on a phone: More >
    Screen preview). Tap it to make it small; tap again to open it.
  - Display settings: font, biggest text size (it shrinks automatically to fit), text colour,
    background colour, wallpaper, wallpaper behind lyrics, darken wallpaper, soft text shadow.

  Video wallpaper (moving background):
  - Display settings > Wallpaper > choose an MP4 video (a picture also works).
  - The video plays silently on a loop. It shows when you press "Church wallpaper", and also
    behind the lyrics if you tick "Show the wallpaper behind the lyrics too".
  - Tick "Darken the wallpaper while lyrics are showing" so the words stay easy to read.
  - Too much movement? Display settings > "Video wallpaper movement": Slow, Very slow, or
    Still (no movement - shows one picture from the video). "Soften the video wallpaper"
    blurs it a little or more, so it becomes a calm background.
  - Best results: MP4, 1920x1080, 30 seconds to 2 minutes long, calm slow motion, under 200 MB.
    Very large videos take longer to upload from a phone.
  - Use Chrome or Edge for the church screen.


5. ADDING AND IMPORTING SONGS
-----------------------------
  New song:
  - Press "New song", type the title.
  - "Search Google for lyrics" opens Google; copy the lyrics there.
  - "Paste copied lyrics" (or Ctrl+V / press-and-hold Paste on a phone).
  - Or "Get lyrics from a web link": open the lyrics page in the browser, copy its link from
    the address bar, press the button and paste the link. The toolkit opens the page, keeps
    only the lyrics (menus, adverts and comments are left out) and fills in the title if empty.
    Read them through before saving. Needs internet on the computer for that moment only.
    Some lyrics sites block this - then copy the lyrics on the page and use "Paste copied lyrics".
  - Or "Read lyrics from pictures": choose one or more screenshots or photos of the song (on a
    phone you can take a photo). The words are read straight into the lyrics box, in order, with
    the stanza gaps kept. A heading at the top becomes the title. Works without internet.
    Always read it through: pictures can give small mistakes (l and I, missing ĩ / ũ marks).
  - Leave ONE EMPTY LINE between stanzas. Line breaks are kept exactly as typed.
  - Press "Save song".
  Song numbers: every song has its own number (the gold round number before its title, and beside the title when the song
  is open). The number is permanent: adding, deleting, importing or restoring songs never changes the number of any
  other song, and a number is never given to a different song. A deleted song that is restored gets its old number back.
  New songs get the next free number.
  Finding a song: type in the search box a NUMBER (12, #12 or no. 12), part of the TITLE, or any WORDS from inside the
  song (capitals, commas and line breaks do not matter). The exact number is shown first; Enter opens the first one.
  Edit / delete: open the song and use Edit or Delete, or right-click (press and hold on a
  phone) a song in the list.
  Quick edits: right-click (press and hold on a phone) a STANZA or the CHORUS to Edit, Cut,
  Delete (or Paste a cut stanza after another one). Right-click the song TITLE to edit it.
  Everything saves by itself as you change it - there is no need to press Save.

  Import songs (from other song programs too):
  - Press "Import songs" and choose many files at once. It reads:
      EasyWorship: Songs.db + SongWords.db (choose both together), .ewsx, RTF exports
      ProPresenter 7 (.pro) and 6 (.pro6)      OpenLP: .xml exports, songs.sqlite, .osz
      OpenSong song files    VideoPsalm (.json)    FreeShow (.show)    EasySlides (.xml)
      Quelea    SongSelect / CCLI (.usr, .txt)    ChordPro (.cho)    SongBeamer (.sng)
      PowerPoint (.pptx - one song per file, each slide a stanza)    Word (.docx)
      Text (.txt), web pages (.html), Prepfix songs files (.json), or a .zip of any of these.
  - Your own song documents - PDF, Word (.docx, and .doc if Word is installed), PowerPoint (.pptx),
    text (.txt): put the song TITLE on its own line, then the stanzas with an EMPTY LINE between
    them. One file can hold many songs: each title on its own line starts the next song.
    Hymn numbers (1. 2. ...) and words like "Chorus" are removed. Bold or Heading lines in Word
    and bigger lines in a PDF are taken as titles. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) are read too.
    Old PowerPoint (.ppt) files: open in PowerPoint, Save As .pptx, then import.
  - Pictures and screenshots of lyrics (.jpg, .png...) can be chosen in Import songs too:
    each picture becomes one song. For a song spread over several screenshots, use
    New song > "Read lyrics from pictures" and choose all of them together.
  - "Songs from other programs" looks on this computer for EasyWorship, OpenLP, ProPresenter,
    FreeShow, OpenSong and VideoPsalm and imports their whole song library with one button.
  - Songs already in the library are skipped.

  Tidy (one uniform look for all songs):
  - Every word starts with a capital letter, the rest small - no more ALL CAPITALS.
  - Leftover HTML, extra blank lines, "Author Unknown", section names (Verse 1, Chorus,
    Kiitikio...) and copyright lines are removed. Stanzas longer than 8 lines are split.
  - New songs, edited songs and imported songs are tidied automatically when saved.
  - "Tidy all songs" does the same to every song already in the library.

  Select several songs: in the Songs tab press "Select". Tick the songs you want, or search
  (e.g. "yesu") and press "Select all". Then choose what to do with all of them at once:
  add to a playlist (or make a new playlist from them), make favourites / remove from favourites,
  tidy, export to a file, share (WhatsApp, email...), or delete. Press "Done" when finished.
  Song folders (kinds of songs - Praise, Worship, Hymns, Swahili, Kikuyu...):
  - Songs tab: the box above the search shows "All songs" or one folder. Choose "+ New folder..."
    to make one. With a folder showing, ✎ renames it and 🗑 deletes it (its songs are kept).
  - Put songs in a folder: Select > tick songs > "Put in a folder". Or open a song and use the
    📁 box under its title. A song is in one folder at a time.
  - Export can export one folder.

  Playlists = the order of service for a day (e.g. "Sunday 9 Nov"):
  - Open the playlist and press "+ Section" for parts like Slow set, Fast set, Praise, Worship,
    Presentation, Choir, Echoes (or type your own), with the name of the lead or group.
  - Sections show as gold headings in the playlist. Move a heading with ↑ ↓, tap it (✎) to change
    or remove it. To put songs under a section: Select > tick songs > "Into section: ...".
  - When a song from the playlist is open, the line under its title shows its section and lead.
  - Next / previous song in the playlist skips over the headings. Share and Export keep them.

  Moving songs (sorting them out):
  - Songs tab > Select > "Move to a playlist": the songs go into that playlist and are taken
    out of any other playlist.
  - Playlists tab > open a playlist > Select: move the ticked songs to another playlist (they
    leave this one), copy them (they stay here too), move them to the top or bottom of this
    playlist, or remove them from it. ↑ ↓ beside each song still move one song at a time.

  Favourites: tap the star beside a song (or on its page) to make it a favourite.
  The "★" button next to the search box shows only favourites (tap again for all songs).
  While favourites are showing, "+ Add all favourites to a playlist..." puts them into a playlist
  (or makes a new playlist from them). Songs already in the playlist are not added twice.
  To export one playlist: Playlists tab > choose the playlist > Export.

  Export songs: choose All songs, Favourites or a playlist, and the kind of file:
  - Prepfix songs file (.json): bring it into any Church Media Toolkit with Import songs.
    Its playlists and favourites come too; songs already there are skipped.
  - Text file (.txt): to read, print or send.
  The file goes to the Downloads folder. A Display settings > Download backup file can also
  be brought back with Import songs.

  Share: open a song and press Share - WhatsApp, Email, Copy, or the phone's share menu.


6. PLAYLISTS
------------
  - Playlists tab > "New playlist" (e.g. "Sunday 3 Nov").
  - Press "+ Add songs", then "+ list" beside each song (press again to remove).
    Or, with a song open, use "+ Add to playlist..." next to its title.
  - In the Playlists tab use the arrows to reorder, X to remove, Rename, Delete, Share
    (shares all song titles and lyrics).
  - When a song is opened from a playlist: on its LAST stanza the button becomes "Next song"
    (with the song's name), and on its FIRST stanza it becomes "Previous song".
    Page Down / Page Up on the laptop do the same.


7. LIVE STREAM IN OBS
---------------------
  - In OBS: Sources > + > Browser.
  - URL:     http://localhost:3000/?view=stream
  - Width 1920, Height 1080 (match your canvas). Leave "Custom CSS" as it is.
  - Tick "Refresh browser when scene becomes active" (optional).
  - The stream shows ONLY the selected line, at the bottom, with a transparent background.
  - Display settings > Stream screen: font, size, colour, and the readability effect
    (black outline, soft shadow, dark box, or none).
  - "Blank stream" button (key S) hides the stream lyrics.
  - If OBS is on a different computer, use the phone address instead of localhost,
    e.g. http://192.168.1.20:3000/?view=stream


KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS (computer)
  Down / Right arrow    next line            S / Page Down   next stanza
  Up / Left arrow       previous line        Page Up         previous stanza
  C                     show the chorus      H               hide / show the church screen
  B                     blank / show stream  W               church wallpaper
  P                     show / close projector


BACKUP
  Display settings > "Download backup" saves a copy of everything. You can also just copy
  data.json to a flash disk. To restore, put the file back as data.json and restart.

MEDIA  (tab "Media")
  Pictures, videos and music:
  - "Add pictures / videos / music": choose pictures, videos or music (several at once is fine).
  - Any video (MP4, MOV, AVI, WMV, MKV, FLV, 3GP, MPEG...) and any music (MP3, M4A, WAV, WMA...)
    can be added. Kinds a browser cannot play are changed to MP4 / MP3 automatically (a progress
    message shows). This uses FFmpeg; if it is not found, copy ffmpeg.exe into the toolkit folder.
    If you choose several pictures you are asked: OK = one slide set, Cancel = separate pictures.
  - Open an item and press "Show this now". Pictures and videos fill the projector screen.
  - Videos: Play, Pause, From start, Sound on / Muted, Loop.
    No projector screen open yet? The video plays right there on the laptop, with sound.
    When the projector screen opens it joins in at the same point and plays the sound;
    the laptop preview goes quiet. Your buttons control both.
    Hide screen / Church wallpaper pause the video; showing again carries on.
    If a video will not play (some phone videos), press "Convert it to MP4 now" under it.
  Background music:
  - Open a music item and press "Play this music". It keeps playing while you show songs,
    slides or pictures. Play / Pause / Stop / Loop; the status line at the top also has
    pause and stop buttons. Like videos, it plays on the laptop until the projector screen opens.
  Slide sets:
  - "Add PowerPoint / PDF / Excel / Word / Publisher": choose the document.
    PDF: every page becomes a slide (works on any laptop).
    Excel, Word, Publisher: Microsoft Office on the laptop turns them into pages, then slides.
    If that Office program is not installed, save the file as PDF in it and add the PDF.
    PowerPoint: choose a .pptx file. Microsoft PowerPoint on this laptop turns every slide
    into a picture (it can take a minute). Without PowerPoint: save the slides as pictures
    (PowerPoint or Google Slides: File > Download/Export as PNG or JPEG) and add the pictures
    together as one slide set.
  - "New text slides": type announcements. Leave an empty line between slides; the first line of
    each slide is its heading. "Edit" changes them later.
  - Tap a slide to show it. Next slide / Previous slide (or the arrow keys) move through the set.
  - Rename or Delete an item with the buttons at the top (deleting also removes its files).
  - The live stream (OBS) shows nothing while media is on, so the camera is not covered.

TOOLS  (tab "Tools")
  - Countdown timer: type the minutes and the words above it (e.g. Service starts in), press Start.
    Pause stops it; Start again carries on; Reset sets it back. It turns red at 00:00.
  - Clock: "Show the time on the projector" shows the time and date big.
    A small clock also sits in the bottom-left corner of the church screen all the time
    (Display settings > "Show a small clock in the bottom-left corner" to turn it off).
  - Alert: type the message (e.g. Car KBX 123A please move it from the gate), choose how long it
    stays, press "Show alert". It appears as a red bar at the top of the projector, over whatever
    is showing. Tick "Also on stream" to put it on the live stream too. "Clear alert" removes it.
  - To leave the timer or clock, tap a song line or show something from Media.

CHURCH AND STREAM CONTROLS (top of the control page)
  - Two rows, one above the other. "Church" shows what is on the projector now, "Stream" shows
    what is going to OBS now.
  - Hide church / Show church: hides the lyrics on the projector (your normal wallpaper shows).
    Key H. The row turns red while it is hidden.
  - Blank stream / Show stream: takes the lyrics off the live stream (camera only). Key S.
    The row turns red while it is blank.

PRACTICE LINKS (for the praise and worship team)
  - Every song can have a link to the song on YouTube, Spotify, Audiomack, SoundCloud or an MP3.
  - Open the song and press "Add practice link", or put it in Edit > Practice link.
    Songs with a link show a small headphone mark in the song list.
  - Press "Play practice song". A small player opens in the corner (at the bottom on a phone)
    and the song plays inside the toolkit while you read the lyrics. You can open other songs and
    the music keeps playing. Lyrics brings you back to that song; the arrows make the player
    bigger or smaller; the ↗ button opens it in the YouTube app or a new tab; ✕ stops it.
  - The player is only on that laptop or phone - it never shows on the church screen or the stream.
  - It needs internet. Some websites do not allow playing inside other programs; then use ↗.
  - During practice at church, team members on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop can open the
    Phone app link on their own phones, pick the song and press Play practice song. (The phone
    page can also control the screens, so only give the link to people you trust.)

TEAM APP (Prepfix Team - for the praise and worship team, on their own phones)
  - Press "Team app" (top of the control page). Do this on ONE computer only.
  - Tick "Share our songs with the team app".
  - Team members: type each member's name and phone number and press "+ Add" ("Paste a list..." for many).
    Only these numbers can open the songs. Press Save.
  - Share the link (or the QR code) in the team's WhatsApp group, with the page "Prepfix Team app - for members.pdf".
  - Each member opens the link, types their registered phone number and MAKES THEIR OWN 4-number PIN (once).
    They stay signed in. One number works on ONE phone. "Install the app on this phone" puts it on the home screen.
  - Changing phone / forgot PIN / someone else used the number: press "Remove sign-in" beside the member;
    they sign in again. A member who leaves: press X beside the name.
  - Members see all the church's songs with the lyrics and the practice links, from anywhere (they need internet).
  - Leaders make a playlist in the app (Slow set, Fast set, Choir with the lead's name), then press
    "Send to church toolkit". When this computer is online it appears under Playlists as
    "<playlist name> (from <leader>)", with a green notice at the top. Sending again updates the same playlist.
  - Members can also add a song that is not in the list (title, lyrics, practice link) and add a practice link
    to a song that has none. New songs go into the folder "From the team" (check them), links are added to songs.
  - New songs and new practice links go to the app by themselves whenever this computer is online.
  - To stop sharing, untick the box and press Save.

UPDATES
  Display settings > About & updates > "Check for updates" (needs internet).
  If a new version is ready, press "Install update now". The toolkit restarts by itself.
  Songs, playlists, settings, wallpaper and the church licence are never changed.
  If anything goes wrong during an update, nothing is changed.

LICENCE AND RENEWAL
-------------------
  The toolkit is licensed to your church by Prepfix and is paid for a year at a time.
  Display settings > About shows your church name, Licence ID and the "paid until" date.
  - 7 days before the date, a notice appears at the top of the control page.
  - After the date there are 7 days of grace (a red notice counts them down).
  - Then the projector and stream show only a Prepfix notice until the licence is renewed.
    Your songs, playlists, media and settings are never lost.
  To renew: pay Prepfix (0720872582). You receive a renewal code (it starts with PFX1.).
  Type or paste it in Display settings > About > Renew (or in the red box) - it works at once,
  without internet. When the computer is online, a renewal from Prepfix can also come in by itself.
  Changing the computer's date does not extend the licence.

SEVERAL PRACTICE LINKS FOR ONE SONG
A song can have more than one practice link (for example a slow and a fast version).
- Open the song and press  ✎ Links  (or Edit, then the Practice links box).
- Put ONE link on each line. You can type a name first, like:  Slow version https://youtu.be/...
- The song then shows a ▶ button for each link. While one is playing, the small list in the player switches to another.
- The praise and worship team sees the same buttons in the Prepfix Team app (upload the new Team app files to GitHub).


THE CONTROL WINDOW AND FULL SCREEN
-----------------------------------
  - The control page opens in a big browser window WITH TABS: press + (or Ctrl+T) to open YouTube or anything
    else beside the toolkit. F11 = full screen and back.
  - The church screen (projector) opens full screen by itself. A church screen opened by hand in a browser: click
    anywhere once for full screen, Esc to leave, F or double-click to come back.
  Long stanzas are cut into smaller stanzas automatically (also when the whole song is one long line).

  SEARCH MANY SONGS AT ONCE: in the search box write numbers, titles or words from the lyrics, separated by commas
  (or ; or +).  Examples:  12, 45, 100      105-110      mwokozi, 33, nyakati zote
  The songs appear in the order you wrote them. "Make a playlist of these" puts them ready to display, one after the other.
  Pasting a list with one song per line works too.

NEW IN THIS VERSION
===================
  The control page opens in a big browser window with tabs (+ or Ctrl+T for a new tab; F11 = full screen).

  Bigger title line: CHURCH MEDIA TOOLKIT and the church name now use the whole top line.

  Top buttons: two tidy lines. Line 1: Wallpaper, Projector, Church screen, Stream screen.
  Line 2: Settings, Phone app, Team app.

  Find many songs at once (Songs tab, and on the phone too):
    1. Press "Find many songs at once - make a playlist" (or paste several lines into the search box).
    2. Write ONE SONG ON EACH LINE: words from the lyrics, a title, or a song number. Example:
           Chakutumaini sina
           wewe mungu wastahili
           1
           23
           kila siku
    3. Every line is checked against your songs. A green tick shows the song found (and the lyric line that matched);
       a red cross means NO song has those words, so nobody needs to search online for it.
    4. If several songs match a line, choose the right one in the small list beside it. Untick a song to leave it out.
    5. Type a playlist name and press "Make playlist and open the first".
